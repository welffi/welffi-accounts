# welffi accounts

log in to your roblox accounts once, then launch any of them together. part of [welffi hub](https://github.com/welffi/welffi-hub)

## what it does

- add accounts by logging in through a browser window. the login gets sealed to your windows user, so it can't be read anywhere else
- tick accounts and open them all, follow a player, join your most recent game, or join a private server
- save private server links by name
- a switch key (f4 by default) jumps between roblox windows, and keeps w a s d, space and shift held
- performance fastflags you can turn on and off
- bring over the accounts and servers from welffi's macros

## how to download

get it from welffi hub, or get `welffi-accounts.exe` from the [latest release](https://github.com/welffi/welffi-accounts/releases/latest) and run it. nothing else needs installing

if you used the account manager in the old hub, your accounts move over by themselves the first time you open it
